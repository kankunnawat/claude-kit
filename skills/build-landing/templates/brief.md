# Site brief: <slug>

Filled in phase 0 through a one-question-at-a-time interview. A portfolio site gets its brief from the portfolio folder instead; this template is for a standalone site.

## Goal
One sentence: what the page must make happen.

## Audience
Who reads it, what they already know, what they are deciding.

## Primary question
The one question the page answers.

## Pages
Landing page plus the secondary pages, one line each with the question it answers.

## Primary action
The single action the page asks for, and where it must stay visible.

## Keyword and indexing policy
Cluster phrase for title and H1; indexing state until release; canonical rules.

## Allowed claims
Only sentences that may be repeated verbatim. Everything else is labeled as an example.

## Tone
Three adjectives and one sentence of what it is not.

## Visual seed
Palette seed, type seed, hero idea. A seed until the owner approves a direction in phase 2.

## Benchmark
Live pages to study for composition, typography, material, lighting, density, pacing, motion. Never their copy, assets, hero geometry, or palette.

## Interaction
Name, purpose, form, states, reset, JS-off content.

## Delivery target
Host, team, project, public address policy, who may deploy, what needs explicit authorization.
