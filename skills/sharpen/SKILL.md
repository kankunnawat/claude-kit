Before starting, silently answer:

1. How would a top expert do this? Their raw sources, their real method, and an outside check that the result is right.
2. What is Kan actually after, beyond the literal words?
3. Is the task over- or underspecified? Drop constraints that don't serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Leave Kan's calls open instead of deciding them.

Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger. Always keep one outside check that the result is right.
Don't narrate this framing and don't hand back a rewritten prompt. Do the work.
For complex, multi-file, or architectural work use superpowers brainstorming instead.
